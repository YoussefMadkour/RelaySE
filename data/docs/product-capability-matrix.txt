Work Intake / Triage
Status: GA
Description: Teams can capture, classify, prioritize, assign, and route incoming work from any channel into one consistent front door.

Cycles / Sprint Planning
Status: GA
Description: Teams can organize approved work into time-boxed cycles, preserve originating context, and track progress against capacity.

Pages / Product Documentation
Status: GA
Description: Teams can create product and technical documentation and link it directly to the execution work it describes.

Jira Import
Status: GA
Description: Existing issues and historical backlog items can be imported from Jira.

Enterprise SSO
Status: GA
Description: Enterprise plans support SSO configuration, including Okta, for production environments.

Advanced Capacity Planning
Status: BETA
Description: Per-engineer capacity calculations and load forecasting are available in controlled beta.
