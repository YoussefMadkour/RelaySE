# RelaySE demo narration

## 1. The problem (0.0s)

A buyer finishes a discovery call. Your sales engineer now has to check product claims, prepare a relevant demo, and coordinate the follow-up. That means repetitive work, and a risk: promising something the product cannot do.

## 2. What RelaySE does (13.9s)

Relay S E is an AI assistant for technical sales teams. It turns a call transcript, customer context, and product capability data into a verified demo plan. Here, Northstar Labs wants to see how incoming requests become planned work.

## 3. The orchestrator + apps (29.7s)

One orchestrator coordinates the workflow. It uses the transcript and saved customer and product data to verify requirements. Plane supplies real product captures. HyperFrames renders the walkthrough. Slack requests review. After approval in Relay, Gmail sends, Calendar schedules, and HubSpot records the result.

## 4. Verify the evidence (49.5s)

The buyer also asks about S A P integration. The product data says it is on the roadmap. Relay blocks it from the demo. Supported workflows are included; unknown capabilities go to human review. Every decision links back to the buyer's words and product evidence.

## 5. Show the result (66.1s)

The plan focuses on three supported workflows: request intake, sprint planning, and linked documentation. Real product screenshots become this personalized walkthrough. The buyer gets a concrete answer to their question, with unsupported claims left out.

## 6. Approve the follow-up (81.8s)

The follow-up then waits for a human. Relay posts a review request to Slack; the sales engineer approves in the app. In this recorded run, approval was followed by a Gmail message with the video, a calendar meeting, and a HubSpot update.

## 7. Why it matters (96.8s)

The result: a relevant demo for the buyer, a clear next step, and less repetitive preparation for the sales team. This prototype uses saved customer and product inputs, real product captures, and live follow-up integrations. Twelve policy tests pass.
